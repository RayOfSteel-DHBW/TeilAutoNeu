# Copy Guardrails

Purpose: Keep all public text aligned with teilAuto tone, accuracy, and legal constraints.

## Tone and Address

- Use Sie-Ansprache and a sachlich, freundlich tone.
- Avoid moralizing, guilt, or poverty signals.
- Do not overpromise or claim capabilities the business does not have.

## Brand and Formatting

- Always write the brand as "teilAuto" (exact casing).
- Phone format must be "07473-922202".
- Use clear, short sentences and avoid jargon.

## Content Accuracy

- No online booking claims, no email contact, no online signup.
- Pricing must stay abstract unless owners confirm exact values.
- Quernutzung is a benefit, not the headline.

## Prohibited Claims

- 24/7 support, app-based booking, free-floating, instant signup, or paperless claims.
- Exact pricing details unless verified for V1 launch.
