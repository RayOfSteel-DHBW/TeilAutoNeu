**Key legal page conventions (from Phase 27/28 learnings):**
- NO `aria-current="page"` on any nav link (legal pages are footer-linked only)
- Full-width content area with `max-w-3xl` for readable line length
- Use `hyphens: auto` CSS property (via inline style `style="hyphens:auto"`) on content container for German compound word wrapping
- Use `text-3xl sm:text-4xl` for page headings (NOT text-5xl — German legal terms like "Datenschutzerklaerung" overflow at 375px)
- Add `overflow-wrap: break-word` on the content container
- Minimal organic blob decoration — just 1 small blob in the header area. Legal pages need subtle Design C identity without distracting from content.
