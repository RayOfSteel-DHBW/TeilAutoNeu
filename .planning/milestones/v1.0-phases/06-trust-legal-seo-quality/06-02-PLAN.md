    - Brief intro: This privacy policy explains what personal data is collected when visiting this website and for what purposes.

    H2 "Server-Logfiles":
    - When visiting the site, the hosting provider (STRATO AG) automatically stores: IP address, date/time, requested page, browser/OS, referrer.
    - Legal basis: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an sicherem Betrieb der Website).
    - Retention: "gemaess den Aufbewahrungsfristen des Hosting-Anbieters" — do NOT speculate on specific durations.

    H2 "Interaktive Karte (OpenFreeMap)":
    - On the Fahrzeuge page, MapLibre GL JS loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org).
    - When the map loads, a connection to OpenFreeMap servers is established, transmitting connection data (including IP address).
    - Per OpenFreeMap's own privacy policy: no cookies, no user tracking, no IP logging by default.
    - Legal basis: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an Kartendarstellung).
    - Note that connection data may be processed on servers outside the EEA.

    H2 "Geplante Webanalyse (kuenftig)":
    - Forward-looking section per CONTEXT.md LOCKED decision.
    - Google Analytics is planned for a future version of this website.
    - Analytics will ONLY activate after explicit user consent via a cookie banner.
    - Legal basis (future): Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO (Einwilligung).
    - Currently: no analytics cookies are set, no user behavior is tracked.

    H2 "Ihre Rechte":
    - Art. 15 DSGVO: Auskunft
    - Art. 16 DSGVO: Berichtigung
    - Art. 17 DSGVO: Loeschung
    - Art. 18 DSGVO: Einschraenkung der Verarbeitung
    - Art. 20 DSGVO: Datenportabilitaet
    - Art. 21 DSGVO: Widerspruch
    - Right to withdraw consent for future cookie analytics.

    H2 "Beschwerderecht":
    - Right to lodge complaint with the supervisory authority:
    - "Landesbeauftragter fuer den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Wuerttemberg"
