## Navigation Structure

### Primary Navigation (menue.htm → right frame)

The navigation is **image-based** with GIF rollover buttons in the left frame. Menu order top-to-bottom:

1. **Vorteile** → `vorteil.html` — "Why carsharing" benefits overview
2. **Allgemeine Infos** → `info.htm` — Detailed carsharing information (biggest content page)
3. **So funktioniert's** → `funktion.htm` — How to book/use the service
4. **Kosten** → `kosten.htm` — Current pricing tables
5. *(Preisvergleich button present but unlinked)*
6. **Stellplätze** → `stellpl.htm` — Parking locations and expansion plans
7. **Fuhrpark** → `auto.htm` — Fleet vehicles with specs
8. **Links** → `link.htm` — Partner organization links
9. *(spacer)*
10. **Kontakt** → `kont.htm` — Opens as **popup window** (280×300px)
11. **Aktuelles** → `aktuell.html` — News (currently empty)
12. **Impressum** → `Impressum.html` — Legal info and privacy policy (text link, not image button)

### Entry Points

- **`index.htm`** — Splash page with logo → click to enter the site
- **`start.htm`** — Main frameset (default content = Vorteile)
- **`startaktuell.htm`** — Alternate frameset (default content = Aktuelles)

### Content Hierarchy

```
Landing (index.htm)
└── Main Site (start.htm frameset)
    ├── For Prospects
    │   ├── Vorteile (why join)
    │   ├── Allgemeine Infos (what is carsharing)
    │   ├── So funktioniert's (how it works)
    │   └── Kosten + Preisvergleich (pricing & comparison)
    ├── Service Details
    │   ├── Stellplätze (where the cars are)
    │   └── Fuhrpark (what cars are available)
    ├── Network
    │   └── Links (partner organizations)
    └── Company
        ├── Kontakt (contact popup)
        ├── Aktuelles (news — empty)
        └── Impressum (legal + privacy)
```

### Cross-Linking

- Pages are **not cross-linked** — navigation is exclusively through the menu frame
- `info.htm` references the logo inline but no links to other pages
- `link.htm` has external links only (carsharing.de, teilAuto-tuebingen.de)
- `kont.htm` has a mailto: link to `info@teilautomoessingen.de`
- No breadcrumbs, no footer navigation, no "back" or "next" links between content pages
